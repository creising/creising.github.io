The fine print

Privacy

Short version: this site doesn't collect much, and I'm not interested in your data. The longer version is below, and it's still pretty short.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. makermayham.coffee is a plain static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and shows no ads. There's no login and no tracking of any kind.

Third parties in the mix

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them. That request is handled under Google's own privacy policy, not mine.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (things like IP addresses and browser type) to serve and protect the site. That's GitHub's process, covered by GitHub's privacy policy.

If you email me

If you send a note to the contact address, I'll have your email address and whatever you wrote. I use it to reply, and nothing else. I don't add you to a list, because there is no list.

The MakerMind app

MakerMind is a separate thing from this website. When it's released it will carry its own privacy policy covering how the app handles data. This page only covers the website.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email my.maker.mayham@gmail.com and I'll sort it out.
